# PepperKey commercialization model

## Product structure

PepperKey should remain one canonical open-source repository and one Flipper Apps Catalog application owned by Ada Tech Solutions Inc. Avoid fragmenting source, marketing, release metadata, and support policy across separate repositories.

The GPL application is free to download, modify, and redistribute under its license. Revenue should come from services and trust-building around the open product, not from restricting recipients' GPL rights.

## Commercial offers after v0.1 acceptance

1. **Compatibility assessment:** a scoped lab report for named OS, browser, relying party, and recovery conditions.
2. **Controlled deployment workshop:** setup, backup-authenticator policy, recovery rehearsal, and operator training for labs or education teams.
3. **Release qualification:** reproducible build, artifact/commit binding, acceptance execution, and evidence package.
4. **Maintainer support:** bounded response targets, issue triage, upgrade assessment, and documented exclusions under a separate agreement.
5. **Sponsorship:** public roadmap support without private forks that conflict with GPL distribution obligations.

Do not sell PepperKey as a certified enterprise authenticator, managed identity platform, sole-account recovery solution, or proprietary per-seat software license.

## Buyer and use-case fit

- Flipper owners who understand legacy U2F limitations.
- Security education and protocol demonstration labs.
- Developers maintaining U2F compatibility paths.
- Internal innovation teams running disposable or non-critical pilots.

Poor fit includes regulated primary authentication, unattended deployments, passkey programs, environments requiring hardware certification/tamper resistance, or services that have removed U2F support.

## Operational readiness

Before accepting money, AdaTech should publish the exact supported matrix, support intake, security-reporting path, open-source notices, warranty disclaimer, data-handling statement, and a clear end-of-support/version policy. Paid scope must identify the tested device, firmware, artifact hash, host/browser/service, recovery owner, and acceptance expiry.

## Pricing framework

Price by scoped outcome rather than per-app license:

- Fixed-fee compatibility assessment.
- Fixed-fee workshop with participant/device limits.
- Milestone-based release qualification.
- Monthly maintainer support with explicit response window and exclusions.

Specific prices, discounts, warranties, and SLAs require AdaTech owner approval and must not be inferred from this repository.
