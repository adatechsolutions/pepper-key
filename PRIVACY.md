# Privacy

PepperKey v0.1 contains no analytics, advertising SDK, network client, account system, or telemetry. It processes U2F requests locally over a directly connected USB host and stores its encrypted identity and counter in its app-specific Flipper data directory.

PepperKey does not intentionally log application hashes, key handles, challenges, device keys, certificate keys, account identifiers, or authentication traffic. Users and issue reporters must not upload those values or app-data files.

The GitHub repository, Flipper Apps Catalog, AdaTech website, video platforms, and future support intake are separate services governed by their operators' privacy practices. Any AdaTech launch page must disclose its own analytics or contact-form handling separately; this app policy does not cover those external services.
