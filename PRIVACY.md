# Privacy

PepperKey contains no analytics, advertising SDK, network client, account system, or telemetry. The accepted v0.1 path processes U2F locally over directly connected USB and stores its encrypted identity/counter in its app-specific Flipper directory.

The 0.2 alpha OTP/challenge/vault code uses published synthetic vectors and creates no live OTP profile, challenge slot, CTAP2 credential, smart-card key, or recovery export. Future capabilities require a privacy update before device acceptance.

PepperKey does not intentionally log application hashes, key handles, challenges, responses, device/private keys, certificate keys, OTP seeds/codes, PINs, account identifiers, recovery data, or authentication traffic. Users and issue reporters must not upload those values or app-data files.

The GitHub repository, Flipper Apps Catalog, AdaTech website, video platforms, and future support intake are separate services governed by their operators' privacy practices. Any AdaTech launch page must disclose its own analytics or contact-form handling separately; this app policy does not cover those external services.
